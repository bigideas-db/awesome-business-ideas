## How the ranking works

| Section | Source | Ranked by | Limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Small business ideas | Main Street Index, 46,000+ US listings | Buyer Fit Score | Asking prices, not closing prices. Earnings are seller-stated. |
| Online businesses | 820+ live listings | Median price-to-profit multiple | Asking prices on one marketplace. |
| Software ideas | Stripe Index, 30,000+ companies | Micro-SaaS density | Companies listed in the Stripe directory, not every company in a market. |
| Online business categories | TrustMRR, 8,000+ products | Share earning $1K+ MRR | Self-listed, so it leans toward founders who share revenue. |
| AI agent ideas | Agent Index, 7,000+ connectors | Readiness verdict | Describes structure, not usage. |
| Complaint ideas | 1M+ complaints | Opportunity score | Model-assisted scoring. Read the reviews before you build. |

## FAQ

### What is the best business idea right now?

By the data, **Chiropractic Clinics**. It ranks #1 of 118 US industries on the BigIdeasDB Buyer Fit Score, with median owner earnings of $200K a year. The right idea for you still depends on budget and skills.

### Which small business makes the most money?

**Civil Engineering** has the highest median owner earnings of any scored industry, at $811K a year.

### Why do owners sell profitable businesses?

Mostly to retire. Of sellers who state a reason, 41% cite retirement and 25% want to focus on other business interests. Very few admit money trouble.

### Is it better to start or buy a business?

Buying gets you customers and cash flow on day one. Starting costs less but takes longer to reach the same earnings. Every small business idea above shows what an established version earns, so you can price both paths.

### What are good SaaS ideas with low competition?

Look where many businesses pay for services but few software products exist. In the Stripe Index that is Marketing Agencies, Nonprofit & Fundraising, Software Development Agencies. AI tools are the opposite: about a third of that market is already micro-SaaS.

### What AI business ideas are still open?

Agent tools for industries AI can't reach yet. In 18 of 54 industries we checked, including restaurant, veterinary clinic, home care agency, physical therapy clinic, a core system has no AI connector.
